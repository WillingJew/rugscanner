PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: April 18, 2026
The short version: We collect your email to run your account and your payment info through Stripe to process subscriptions. We don't sell your data, we don't track your trading activity, and we don't store a history of the tokens you scan.

1. Who we are

RugScanner Pro ("we", "us", "the extension") is a Chrome browser extension that analyzes Solana token holder distributions on trade.padre.gg to help users detect rug pull patterns. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what we don't do with it.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@rugscannerpro.com.

2. What we collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • A password (stored as a salted hash — never in plain text)

Accounts are managed through Supabase, our authentication provider.

Subscription information

If you subscribe to the paid tier, payment is processed by Stripe. We never see or store your credit card details — Stripe handles all payment data directly. We only receive and store your subscription status (active, canceled, past due) so we know whether to grant unlimited scans.

Scan activity

When you run a scan, the extension reads the publicly visible holder list shown on trade.padre.gg and sends that data to our backend for analysis. The backend returns a risk score and flags.

We do not store a history of the tokens you scan. We do not log which contract addresses you've looked at. We track your total scan count only to the extent needed to enforce the 5-scan free tier limit.

Technical information

Standard server logs (IP address, request timestamps, error traces) are generated by our hosting provider for security, abuse prevention, and debugging. These are retained for a short period and are not used to build a profile of your activity.

3. What we don't collect

  • Your Solana wallet address or private keys — we never ask for either
  • Your trades, positions, or portfolio on trade.padre.gg or anywhere else
  • Your browsing history or activity on other websites
  • Your credit card or banking details
  • A log of which tokens you've personally scanned

4. How we use what we collect

We use the limited data we collect for a narrow set of purposes:

  • Running your account and signing you in
  • Processing your subscription and enforcing your plan's scan limits
  • Sending transactional email (account confirmations, password resets, billing notices) via Resend
  • Keeping the service running, debugging issues, and preventing abuse

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

5. Third-party services

RugScanner Pro relies on a small number of third-party providers to operate:

  • Supabase — authentication and account storage
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Resend — transactional email
  • Railway — backend hosting
  • Helius — on-chain Solana data used to analyze holder distributions
  • Anthropic — AI-powered verdict generation (when the AI tier is used)

Each of these providers has their own privacy policy. We only share the minimum data needed for each service to do its job (for example, Stripe receives your payment info but not your scan activity; Helius receives token addresses but not your email).

6. Permissions the extension uses

RugScanner Pro requests the minimum browser permissions needed to function:

  • Access to trade.padre.gg — required to read the holder list on the page you're viewing. The extension only activates on this domain.
  • Storage — required to keep you signed in between sessions and cache your subscription status locally.

The extension does not read, modify, or collect data from any other website you visit.

7. Data security

Passwords are hashed and salted. Connections between the extension, our backend, and all third-party providers are encrypted over HTTPS. We follow reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, though no system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your email and account record from our systems within 30 days. Stripe retains a record of completed transactions as required by law — this is outside our control.

9. Your rights

You can request to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time by emailing support@rugscannerpro.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR (EU) or CCPA (California) — we'll honor those requests regardless of where you're located.

10. Children

RugScanner Pro is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy materially, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top and, for significant changes, notify active users by email. Continued use of the extension after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions, concerns, data requests, or anything else — email support@rugscannerpro.com.
